- **Clear and admit change the subscriber count and the slot under one lock (#2836).** An
  unsubscribe (or a `:subscribers[N]` clear) and a subscribe now change a vertex's delivering-edge
  count and its subscriber slot under one shared map hold, the hold suspend/resume and link
  eviction already take, so a retire always sees a count that equals the live edges and leaves
  the vertex and its descendants at zero. An admission lands only on the registration it was
  decided against: a vertex retired since, registered again or not, takes no edge and the
  admission answers as one retired during the gate does, so a retired vertex carries no edges
  into a later registration. The private `note_subscriber_added` / `note_subscriber_removed`
  pair is now one `note_subscribers(v, delta)` that its caller runs under that hold. No public
  signature, wire byte or `sizeof` changes.
