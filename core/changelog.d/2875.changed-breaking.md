- **A remote subscription keeps one block, drawn from the router's link plane (#2875).** What a
  peer's `:subscribers[]` append keeps for the life of the subscription (the `SUBSCRIBER` TLV, the
  return route and the completed reverse chain) is now ONE block that the edge holds three windows
  into, where it was two or three segments. A router that is not given `router_planes_t::retained`
  draws it from `router_planes_t::link_src`, the long-lived link plane, instead of `flat`, so a
  node that bounds and counts its link plane bounds and counts its subscriptions. A refused block
  answers the subscribe `BACKPRESSURE` and binds nothing; a refused reverse chain no longer
  degrades on its own, because it is the same block. `router_planes_t::retained` and the
  `retained` argument of `graph::op_resolver_t`'s constructor are now `mem::block_source_t*`
  (a `mem::mem_backend_t*` still converts) and are drawn through `try_alloc`: a backend that
  overrides only `alloc` refuses there, and its subscribes answer `BACKPRESSURE`.
