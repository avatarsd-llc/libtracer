- **An oversize TLV length is `FRAME_INVALID` (#2885).** This aligns the codec with RFC-0001 §C.3
  and RFC-0002's `tr::frame::invalid`. `parse_header` has a compile-time bound, the new
  `config_t::kMaxTlvBytes` (16 MiB by default; a NARROW build may lower it in its override
  fragment). When a TLV does not fit the bytes at hand, a declared `length` above the bound is now
  `FRAME_INVALID`, and any other length is still `FRAME_TRUNCATED`. A TLV that fits decodes as
  before, whatever its length.
