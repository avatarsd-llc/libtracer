- **An oversize TLV length is `FRAME_INVALID` (#2885).** This aligns the codec with RFC-0001 §C.3
  and RFC-0002's `tr::frame::invalid`. `parse_header` refuses a declared `length` above the new
  compile-time `config_t::kMaxTlvBytes` as `FRAME_INVALID` on every header, whether or not the
  TLV's bytes are all present. The default is 16 MiB; a NARROW build may lower it in its override
  fragment, never below `0xFFFF` (a `static_assert` holds that). A short TLV at or under the bound
  is still `FRAME_TRUNCATED`.
