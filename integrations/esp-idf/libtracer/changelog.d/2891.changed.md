- **`httpd_ws_link_t`: a quiet session no longer waits for another session's flood (#2891).** Every
  frame the link writes is now one `sendmsg`, header and payload together, so a small reply is one
  TCP segment instead of two. The Nagle switch a flooding drain used to apply is gone: under load
  from other sessions it was handed to whichever session was read last, quiet ones included, and
  each of their replies then waited for a delayed ACK. The ADR-0085 drain budget is now counted per
  session: a session waits for its core to idle only once it has read its share of the budget (the
  budget divided among the sessions that read in the drain), or once the in-call replies written
  to it reach half lwIP's segment queue or a quarter of its send buffer. The frames read between
  two idle moments stay within about one budget plus one frame per session.
  `CONFIG_LIBTRACER_WS_SERVER_RX_DRAIN_FRAMES` is now limited to 0–254 (it was 0–4096), because a
  session's drain count is one byte. With both budgets set, one count bounds frames and bytes
  together, so a drain of mixed frames can end slightly before either budget alone would end it.
