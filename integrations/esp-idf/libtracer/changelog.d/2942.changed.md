- **One session's in-call replies no longer hold other sessions back (#2942).** The httpd
  WebSocket link bounds the in-call replies it writes to one session in one drain at what lwIP
  holds unacknowledged for it (a quarter of the send buffer, or half the segment queue). Past
  that bound the session's next read parked the drain, and so the shared httpd task, until the
  core idled, which delayed every other session's reads and replies. Now the bound only decides
  how a reply travels: the replies past it go out through the session's own queue, written
  without waiting and resumed on the link's own events, as a reply from another task already
  does. A reply that finds that session's control lane full is refused and counted
  (`enqueue_drops`). The drain still parks on its frame and byte budgets; replies no longer
  count toward a park.
