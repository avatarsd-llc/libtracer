- **`httpd_ws_link_t::stats_t::work_post_refused` counts refused work submits (#2943, #2954).**
  When ESP-IDF refuses to queue the link's work (`httpd_queue_work` fails, typically on a full
  control queue), the refusal was not counted anywhere. It now is, for the link's flush and for
  the auth sweep `sweep` posts for sessions past the application's authentication deadline,
  appended as the sixteenth 32-bit tally of `stats_t`. Behaviour is unchanged: a refused submit
  loses no frame, held frames go out on the next submit the server accepts, expired sessions are
  closed by the next `sweep`, and the count is not part of `drop_stats()`. A steadily rising count
  means the server's control queue is too short for its load.
