- **`httpd_ws_link_t::stats_t::flush_post_refused` counts refused flush submits (#2943).** When
  ESP-IDF refuses to queue the link's flush work (`httpd_queue_work` fails, typically on a full
  control queue), the refusal was not counted anywhere. It now is, appended as the sixteenth
  32-bit tally of `stats_t`. Behaviour is unchanged: a refused submit loses no frame, the held
  frames go out on the next submit the server accepts, and the count is not part of
  `drop_stats()`. A steadily rising count means the server's control queue is too short for its
  load.
