- **IDF's PONG and CLOSE echoes follow the link's send rules (#2944).** On a WebSocket session
  whose writes are stalled, an echo ESP-IDF writes itself no longer waits out the send bound: it
  is offered the socket without waiting and skipped whole when nothing is taken, so a peer that
  stopped reading and keeps sending PINGs costs the httpd task one send bound in all, then closes
  through `sweep`. An echo whose header went out and whose payload did not now closes the session
  as a torn stream, instead of leaving a header on the wire that no payload follows.
